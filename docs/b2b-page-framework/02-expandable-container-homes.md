### Engineering Details
- **Wall panels:** 50mm / 75mm PU or fireproof Rockwool sandwich panels
- **Floor frame:** Heavy galvanized steel sub-structure with marine-grade board and vinyl flooring options
- **Electrical / plumbing:** Pre-wired circuits configurable for US NEC, Canadian CEC, or AS/NZS requirements

### FAQ
1. **How long does installation take?** Most 20ft units set up in about two hours with a small crew; 40ft units typically need a crane and about four hours.
2. **Can layouts be customized?** Yes. Bedroom count, bathroom count, facade, and finishes can be adapted to project plans.
3. **Do you support US / Australia electrical standards?** Yes. Localized UL / SAA component options are available by market.
